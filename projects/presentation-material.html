Project Overview

Objective

Identify the 10 most critical UX issues across the selected website and propose design changes that could be validated through A/B or multivariate testing. The goal of the presentation was to communicate the recommended tests clearly enough for a client, stakeholder group, or leadership team to approve moving forward with development and testing.

My Role

UX researcher and presentation designer responsible for auditing the website, prioritizing usability issues, developing test concepts, and presenting recommendations in a stakeholder-ready format.

Tools

Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, Google Slides, cross-browser testing, Silktide, WAVE, Lighthouse, and presentation storytelling.

Deliverables

Compiled research documentation, test control screenshots, test-version mockups, 18-slide presentation, and recorded video walkthrough.

Process

Approach

  1. Website Review: Evaluated the selected website to identify usability, accessibility, and other issues that could affect user completion.
  2. Issue Prioritization: Ranked the 10 most critical UX issues based on user impact, visibility, and potential business value.
  3. Control Documentation: Captured screenshots of the existing page areas to establish the control version for each proposed test.
  4. Test Concept Design: Created mockups showing revised test versions in full-page context, including A/B test options and multivariate variables.
  5. Stakeholder Presentation: Organized findings into a clear presentation explaining the issue, hypothesis, proposed design change, and testing rationale.

Results

Outcomes

  • Delivered a stakeholder-ready UX test presentation that clearly prioritized the most critical website issues.
  • Translated usability findings into actionable A/B and multivariate test concepts supported by control and test mockups.
  • Improved communication of design rationale by connecting each proposed change to user impact and KPIs.
